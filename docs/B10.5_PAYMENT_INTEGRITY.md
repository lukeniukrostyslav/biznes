# B10.5 — Payment integrity checkpoint

## Implemented
Payment creation/editing now enforces that the selected payment client matches the linked invoice client. The payment client is also normalized to the invoice client when the invoice already defines it.

## Why
This closes a financial relationship gap in the lifecycle:
Proposal → Project → Invoice → Payment.

The payment amount is still checked against the invoice's remaining balance, excluding the payment currently being edited.

## Competitive context
Current competitor workflows increasingly connect proposals, projects, invoices and payments in one flow.
