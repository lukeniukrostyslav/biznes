# Business OS - Product Vision

## Positioning

Run your freelance business from one private workspace.

## Core promise

Turn the commercial lifecycle into one connected system:

**Lead -> Proposal -> Project -> Invoice -> Payment -> Profit -> Repeat**

## Primary user

- Freelancer
- Consultant
- Solo service provider
- Small agency owner

## Core value

Business OS connects sales, delivery and money instead of treating them as separate spreadsheets and templates.

## Planned modules

1. Sales Pipeline - lead stages, opportunity value and next action.
2. Client CRM - profiles, value, projects, notes and follow-ups.
3. Projects - scope, deadline, hours, expenses, revenue, profit, margin and progress.
4. Pricing Engine - minimum, target and premium rates.
5. Proposal Builder - structured client proposals.
6. Scope & Profit Calculator - project economics before accepting work.
7. Invoice Builder - line items, tax and payment terms.
8. Follow-up System - proposals, payments and next client actions.
9. Cashflow - expected, received and outstanding revenue.
10. Business Dashboard - revenue, profit, pipeline, clients, projects and follow-ups.

## Product boundaries

Not included: Notion, course content, generic prompt packs, PDF-only delivery, mandatory SaaS subscription, mandatory backend, or guaranteed-income claims.

## Commercial model

Target one-time purchase: $79-$99. This is a product target, not a guarantee of sales.

## Quality bar

Fast, clean, professional, responsive, understandable without a tutorial, safe local persistence, export/import backup, consistent localization, and no broken production controls.
